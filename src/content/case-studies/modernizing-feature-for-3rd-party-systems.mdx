Legacy architecture of iCE player cards prevented us from opening up this feature for a new white labeling project.

The immediate demands from the product team is to convert the player card modal into a linkable page. The quick solution to open a new window for the player card gave us the time and opportunity to review existing user feedback for this feature.

## User Feedback
- Player search load time is unacceptably long
- Visualizations use an unconventional scale
- Visualizations are confusing
- Stats tables are hard to read

## Proposed solutions
- Added a league filter for player searching to drastically reduce load time (backend resources were not available for this project)
- Add the existing watchlist feature to the player search to avoid hefty load times and quicker access to players of interest
- Unified the pages visualization and scale to match the principles of the new Table Design System
- Created visual hierarchy in visualizations to improve user experience
- Updated the nav to include a new Player Card section that will allow users to search for players as well as act as the page where users can view the player cards
