The Sportlogiq operations team use to manage all of its tasks on google spreadsheets and tools designed by engineers for engineers. OneApp allows the operations team to manage their work without the need for any engineering involvement.

I joined as a junior UX designer when OneApp was just launched. It started as a database of all the games with basic functions and over the years grew to encompass roster management, video capture and ingestion, resolving player database issues, validating season schedules, video annotator training, generating homographies for soccer games and much much more! 

I designed the UI/UX for almost every single feature and slowly but surely improved the design system. Here's a small assortment:
